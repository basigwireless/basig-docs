# About Us

<p class="bw-lead">BaSig Wireless Laboratories turns cutting-edge wireless research into reliable, affordable, industry-ready products.</p>

Founded by [**Dr. Prabhu Chandhar**](https://bio.basigwireless.com) in Mayiladuthurai, Tamil Nadu, BaSig solves challenging problems in telecommunications through **research, consultancy and product development**.
